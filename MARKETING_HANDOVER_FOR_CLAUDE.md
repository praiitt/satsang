## 1. Product Context & Overview
**Satsang** is an innovative AI-powered spiritual guidance platform that enables users to connect with a virtual spiritual guru (Guruji) through real-time voice conversations in Hindi. Built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), it requires no installation.

### Key Value Proposition
"Your spiritual guide, in your language, on demand."

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** Hindi-first users 25–55 in India who practice bhakti; daily commuters; homemakers; students seeking calm/discipline.
- **Secondary:** NRI diaspora 30–60, Seniors 55+, Devotional music listeners.

### Core Features
1. **AI-Powered Spiritual Guru:** Conversational AI that understands Hindi (Romanized) and provides personalized spiritual guidance.
2. **Voice-First Experience:** Real-time STT and TTS in Hindi. Hands-free interaction.
3. **Devotional Content:** Play bhajans from YouTube on demand.
4. **Vani/Pravachan:** Searchable spiritual discourses and highlights.
5. **No App Install:** Fast browser PWA that loads in seconds with a 15-minute free trial.

### Multi-Agent Architecture
The app contains multiple specialized AI agents:
- **Guruji:** Spiritual Q&A
- **Osho:** Radical Wisdom
- **Music Agent:** Devotional Music (Bhajans)
- **Hinduism Agent:** Dharma Knowledge
- **Vedic Jyotish:** Astrology & Kundli
- **Tarot Agent:** Mystical Guidance
- **Meditation Agent:** Inner Peace & Breathing
- **Trance Music Guide:** Ambient Soundscapes

## 3. Marketing & Content Distribution Strategy
**The Big Idea:** Use the app's own AI agents as content factories. Every session, bhajan, kundli reading, and meditation is raw material for our content strategy.

### Content Formats
- **Short-Form Video (YouTube Shorts/Reels):** "Ask Guruji" Q&A (30-60s), Daily Rashifal with animated chart, Bhajan of the Day.
- **Audio:** Daily Satsang Podcast, Guided Meditation Series, Bhajan Jukebox.
- **Written/Social:** Daily Shloka + Meaning, Festival Guides, "Did You Know?" Dharma facts.

### Acquisition Channels
- **Organic:** YouTube Shorts/Reels, SEO on Hindi queries ("भक्ति क्या है?"), WhatsApp share flows.
- **Paid:** Meta/YouTube ads targeting bhakti interest clusters, Search ads on Hindi queries.
- **Partnerships:** Temple QR codes, devotional musicians, festival activations.

### Activation Funnel
Impression → Landing Page → Tap Demo → Phone Sign-in → Mic Permission → 1st Successful Response → Share to WhatsApp or "Add to Home".
